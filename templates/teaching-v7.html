Courses and teaching materials

I began teaching economics at CY Cergy Paris Université in 2019. I have taught lectures and tutorials in a range of subjects, including microeconomics, macroeconomics, data analysis, and econometrics, from undergraduate to doctoral level. This page presents selected materials for my courses in the 2026/2027 academic year. My teaching connects economic intuition, formal reasoning, and empirical evidence, with an emphasis on explaining why a method works and interpreting results carefully.

Teaching approach

Three principles that structure a class

01

Clarify the core idea

Begin with one durable objective and use short checks to make understanding visible.

02

Explain why, not only how

Students should be able to state the assumptions, intuition, and consequences of failure.

03

Interpret evidence responsibly

Separate description, identification, and policy judgment in every empirical exercise.
