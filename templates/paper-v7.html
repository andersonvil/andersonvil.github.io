Why is reported childcare time transformed?

Passive childcare is supervision or availability that can coincide with other activities, including sleep; a report close to 168 hours is not 168 hours of active care or a direct opportunity-cost measure. Before transformation, reported time is bounded so childcare plus market work does not exceed 119.7 waking hours per week. Constants kᴵ are calibrated so adjusted PSID means match published ATUS primary-care averages.

tᴵadj = tᴵPSID − kᴵ tanh(tᴵPSID / kᴵ)

The correction preserves zeros and approaches a constant downward shift at high reports, but it changes the distribution and potentially the joint distribution across spouses. Adjusted PSID hours are the reference estimation measure; ATUS is the calibration benchmark.
