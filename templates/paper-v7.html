Why reported childcare time is transformed

PSID reports can include simultaneous and sleep-time care and reach the 168-hour weekly ceiling. The manuscript uses a smooth adjustment calibrated to ATUS averages:

tᵢᵃᵈʲ = tᵢᴾˢᴵᴰ − kᵢ × tanh(tᵢᴾˢᴵᴰ / kᵢ)

It preserves zero, approaches a constant downward shift for large reports, and lowers sample means from 35.12 to 9.66 hours for fathers and from 53.65 to 18.97 for mothers. It changes the distribution as well as the mean, so robustness checks remain essential.

{% endif %}
