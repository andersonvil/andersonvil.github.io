Teaching approach

Three principles that structure a class

01

Clarify the core idea

Each session begins with one durable objective and uses short formative checks to make understanding visible.

02

Explain why before how

Methods become transferable when students can state the assumptions, intuition, and consequences of failure.

03

Interpret evidence responsibly

Exercises connect formal tools to real questions while separating description, identification, and policy judgment.
